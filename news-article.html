First-ever BADRC-SIAC Conference on Trends and Practices in International Arbitration

By BADRC Secretariat · 6–7 May 2026

The SIAC-BADRC Conference and Training Programme was held over two days in Thimphu on 6 and 7 May 2026, marking a significant milestone in Bhutan's journey toward establishing a robust arbitration ecosystem. The event brought together international experts from the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC): Mr. Ng Kim Beng, Member of the SIAC Court of Arbitration and Managing Partner, Rajah & Tann Singapore LLP; Ms. Schwetha Budhuri, Director and Head (South Asia), SIAC; and Ms. Snigdha Bhatta, Counsel at SIAC.

The first day featured a half-day panel discussion on the theme "Trends and Practices in International Arbitration," moderated by Ms. Snigdha, with panel members Mr. Kim Beng, Ms. Schwetha, Dasho Pema Wangchuk (Justice of the Supreme Court of Bhutan), Dasho Tenzin (Secretary General of BADRC), and Ms. Kunzang Tshomo Dukpa (Founder and Managing Partner at Bhutanese Legal Solutions). The one-hour discussion touched on Bhutan's arbitral landscape and its evolution to the current ecosystem, while constantly referencing the international standard of arbitral proceedings set by SIAC. The rest of the day was followed by training on major international treaties and frameworks that govern international arbitration, the core principles of international arbitration, an introduction to SIAC, the arbitration lifecycle, and the SIAC Rules 2025, delivered by the foreign trainers.

The second day covered the same training, but for judges of Bhutan. For the first time in Bhutan's arbitration history, a dedicated full-day training session was conducted specifically for sitting judges — reflecting Bhutan's aim to have the judiciary well versed in pro-arbitration principles, particularly judicial deference, minimal interference, and enforcement of foreign awards under the New York Convention.
