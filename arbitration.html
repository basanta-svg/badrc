Arbitration

Binding, enforceable resolution for commercial disputes.

BADRC administers domestic and international arbitrations under its own rules, providing case management, panel access, and emergency/interim relief procedures from commencement to award.

Overview

Domestic arbitration

For disputes between parties within Bhutan, BADRC provides administered arbitration under its domestic rules, with access to a roster of qualified Bhutanese and, where relevant, international arbitrators.

International arbitration

Commencement Guidance

How to commence an arbitration

Submit a Notice of Arbitration

File the Notice of Arbitration together with the required fee, per the applicable BADRC Arbitration Rules.

Constitution of the tribunal

The tribunal is constituted per the parties' agreement or, absent agreement, the applicable Rules and BADRC's appointment procedure.

Case management conference

The tribunal and parties agree a procedural timetable for the conduct of proceedings.

Hearing and award

Following submissions and any hearing, the tribunal renders a final, binding award.

Model Clauses

Standard BADRC arbitration clause

Parties wishing to submit future disputes under a contract to BADRC arbitration are recommended to adopt the following clause.

"Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this contract, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Bhutan Alternative Dispute Resolution Centre (BADRC) in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of BADRC for the time being in force.

The seat of arbitration shall be [Thimphu, Bhutan]. The number of arbitrators shall be [one/three]. The language of the arbitration shall be [English]."
