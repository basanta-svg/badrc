About BADRC

A national institution for independent dispute resolution.

Established under the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of Bhutan, 2013, BADRC administers arbitration and mediation with impartiality, efficiency and international credibility.

Mandate & Legal Basis

Our statutory foundation

BADRC is established as a statutory body under the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of Bhutan, 2013, with the mandate to administer arbitration and mediation for disputing parties, businesses, government agencies and international partners. The Centre operates independently in the exercise of its case-administration functions.
