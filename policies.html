BADRC is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website.

1. Information we collect

Personal data you provide: When you contact us, register an account, or subscribe to our services, we may collect personal information such as your first and last name, email address, phone number, and postal address.

Usage data: We automatically collect information when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visit, and diagnostic data.

2. How we use your information

We use your personal data to: provide and maintain our service; manage your account and registration; contact you with updates or security information; manage your requests and inquiries; and analyze usage trends and improve our service.

3. Data sharing

We may share your information with service providers, affiliates, or business partners who assist us in operating our website and providing our services. We require these parties to honor this Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

5. Data security

We implement reasonable security safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Contact us at [Contact Email] to exercise these rights.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

No confidential case data is ever collected via public-facing forms on this site.

BADRC uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this notice.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

How we use cookies

Necessary cookies: essential for the website to function properly. Functional cookies: enable enhanced functionality and personalization. Analytical cookies: help us understand how visitors interact with our site to improve performance.

Managing your preferences

You can manage cookie settings through your browser or our cookie banner. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

In accordance with our core value of sustainability, BADRC maintains a rigorous document-retention policy to ensure that solutions are practical and enduring.

Case records: official case files, including arbitral awards and settlement agreements, are retained in secure, confidential archives for the period required by the ADR Act to support long-term legal certainty.

Integrity of data: our record-keeping systems are designed to reflect stability, ensuring that institutional data is retrievable, accurate, and protected against unauthorized access.
