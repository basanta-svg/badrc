Bhutan Alternative Dispute Resolution Centre

Independent resolution, rooted in Bhutan

BADRC administers domestic and international arbitration and mediation under the Alternative Dispute Resolution Act of Bhutan, 2013 — providing disputing parties, businesses and their counsel with an efficient, impartial forum, and connecting Bhutan to the wider international ADR community.

Start an Arbitration Start Mediation

Arbitration

Binding resolution for commercial and cross-border disputes

From commencement to award, BADRC administers arbitrations under its own rules, with access to a panel of qualified domestic and international arbitrators and emergency/interim relief procedures.

Explore Arbitration

Mediation & Negotiated Settlement

Facilitated, consensual settlement of disputes

BADRC's mediation service helps parties reach durable, enforceable settlements through a structured, confidential process guided by accredited mediators.
