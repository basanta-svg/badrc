Mediation & Negotiated Settlement

Consensual settlement, guided by accredited mediators.

BADRC's mediation service helps parties reach durable, enforceable settlements through a structured, confidential process — often faster and less adversarial than arbitration or litigation.

Overview

Our mediation services

Mediation and Negotiated Settlement at BADRC are voluntary and confidential processes designed to help parties move beyond deadlock toward mutually acceptable resolutions. While arbitration focuses on a binding determination, our mediation services emphasize flow — the restorative power of dialogue, where hardened positions soften and communication begins to circulate again. These services are ideal for parties who wish to resolve their differences in a way that is less adversarial, more cost-effective, and deeply protective of ongoing professional or personal relationships.

Commencement Guidance

How to commence mediation

Starting a mediation is an invitation to transition from hostility to harmony. To commence a process:

Initial inquiry

Contact the BADRC Registrar to discuss whether your dispute is suitable for mediation or a supported negotiated settlement.

Request for Mediation

Submit a formal request form. If the other party has not yet agreed to mediate, the Centre can act as a Messenger of Peace (the Magpie) to invite them into the process.

Agreement to mediate

Once both parties consent, the Registrar facilitates the signing of a formal Mediation Agreement, ensuring all participants understand the ground rules of confidentiality and neutrality.

Model Clauses

Mediation only

In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this contract, or the breach thereof, the parties agree first to try in good faith to settle the dispute by mediation administered by the Bhutan ADR Centre, in accordance with their Mediation Rules ("BADRC Mediation Rules") for the time being in force, before resorting to arbitration, litigation, or some other dispute resolution procedure.

The Mediation shall consist of sole/ _____ numbers of Mediators.

The seat of Mediation shall be…………………………………………………………

The Language of the Mediation shall be _________.

The mediation process and settlement Agreement, if any, shall be governed by, construed and take effect in accordance with the substantive law of __________.

Mediation and arbitration

For parties who wish mediation to be the first step, with arbitration as the fallback if settlement is not reached.

In the event of a dispute arising out of or relating to this contract, including any question regarding its existence, validity or termination, the parties shall first seek settlement of that dispute by mediation in accordance with the Bhutan ADR Rules, which Rules are deemed to be incorporated by reference into this clause.

If the dispute is not settled by mediation within [ …….] days of the commencement of the mediation, or such further period as the parties shall agree in writing, the dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the BADRC Arbitration Rules, which Rules are deemed to be incorporated by reference into this clause.

The language to be used in the mediation and in the arbitration shall be [ ].
The governing law of the contract shall be the substantive law of [ ].

In any arbitration commenced pursuant to this clause:
(a) the number of arbitrators shall be [one/three]; and
(b) the seat, or legal place, of arbitration shall be [City and/or Country].
